The job descriptionwhat the app read

Road Tek, part of the Department of Transport and Main Roads, is seeking an experienced ICT Project Manager to lead the delivery of an In-Vehicle Management System across its fleet and depots.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead the end to end delivery of the in-vehicle management system, from planning through to rollout across vehicles and depots.
  • Manage vendors, integration partners and internal stakeholders to deliver telematics and field device capability.
  • Maintain project governance, risk management, budget and reporting in line with Queensland Government standards.
  • Coordinate testing, training and change activities to support field operations and depot staff.

Essential

  • Demonstrated experience delivering ICT projects in a government or large enterprise environment.
  • Strong stakeholder and vendor management.
  • Experience with systems integration and hardware or device rollouts is highly regarded.

The successful applicant will be Brisbane based, with some travel to depots as required.
